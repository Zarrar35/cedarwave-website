Roofmates — Privacy

Roofmates Privacy Policy

Roofmates by Cedarwave Technologies helps adults coordinate shared living, including household membership, shared expenses and settlements, invitations, Events, Pools, receipt attachments, and participant-scoped conversations. This policy explains how IQBAL, LLC d/b/a Cedarwave Technologies handles information through the Roofmates apps, websites, and related services.

Information we collect

CategoryExamples and uses
Account and contact informationName, email, authentication-provider subject, and internal identifiers used for sign-in, account security, recovery, and support.
Household and profile informationHousehold name, membership, role, profile image, and preferences used to provide shared-living features and permissions.
Shared financial recordsExpense details, amounts, participants, confirmations, shares, obligations, settlements, disputes, and audit history used to maintain the shared ledger.
User contentNotes, selected images, messages when server sync is enabled, Event and Pool content, chores, lists, and support correspondence.
Device and diagnostics informationInstallation and push-token details, app/OS version, crash and performance data, request identifiers, IP address, and security signals when the corresponding production integration is enabled.
PurchasesProduct, order, subscription, entitlement, refund, and dispute status when paid features are enabled. Roofmates does not receive raw app-store or hosted-payment card credentials.

No advertising sale or tracking

Roofmates does not sell personal information, serve third-party behavioral ads, or track users across other companies’ apps and websites for advertising. Roofmates does not use an advertising identifier and does not request a device address book, precise location, health information, or raw card or bank credentials.

Product improvement, new features, and AI

Roofmates may use service records and diagnostics to operate, secure, support, evaluate, and improve existing features and to plan new ones. Roofmates does not use identifiable private messages, receipt images, household data, financial records, or support correspondence to train general-purpose or Roofmates-specific AI models by default.

Aggregate statistics and information de-identified before analysis may be used for reliability, feature evaluation, and product planning. Roofmates does not attempt to re-identify that information. Any future use of identifiable or user-provided content for model training, fine-tuning, or evaluation-set creation requires a separate, specific, default-off opt-in that identifies the data, purpose, provider, retention, and withdrawal effect. A change to this policy alone is not consent to model training.

How information is shared

Information is shared with the household, Event, Pool, transaction, or participant audience selected by the product rules; with contracted service providers needed for authentication, infrastructure, diagnostics, payments, messaging, and support; when required for law and safety; or as part of a business transfer subject to applicable protection.

Retention and deletion

Roofmates keeps information only for as long as reasonably needed to provide and secure the service, maintain records participants rely on, resolve disputes, comply with law, and enforce agreements. Retention depends on the record and may also be limited by an approved legal or security hold.

Request deletion in the app from the You tab by selecting Delete account, or through the public deletion page. Open shared obligations do not prevent deletion. After confirmation, Roofmates removes or de-identifies direct account information, retires device installations, and queues deletion of the associated authentication account and private receipt objects.

Open or settled shared financial, settlement, dispute, audit, security, and legally required records may remain under the non-identifying “Deleted user” label when other participants rely on that history. Encrypted recovery copies may retain deleted information until their normal backup cycle expires. Retained records are not used for advertising or model training.

Security

Roofmates uses safeguards including TLS in transit, managed encrypted storage, restricted database roles, least-privilege access, encrypted push tokens, signed provider webhooks, audit logs, rate limiting, secret management, and monitored recovery processes. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and rights

Depending on location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, port, restrict, or object to processing, withdraw consent, or appeal a request decision. Use the in-app controls or email roofmates-support@cedarwavetechnologies.com. We may verify identity before fulfilling a request.

Children and international processing

Roofmates is not directed to children under 13, and the Terms require users to be at least 18 or the age of legal majority where they live. Information may be processed in the United States and other countries where approved service providers operate.
