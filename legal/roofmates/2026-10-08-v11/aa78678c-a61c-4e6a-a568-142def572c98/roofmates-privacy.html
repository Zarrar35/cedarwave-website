Privacy Policy

Effective 10 October 2026 at 04:35:00 UTC. This is an archived, versioned policy document.

Operator: CedarWave Technologies. Privacy contact: roofmates-support@cedarwavetechnologies.com. Account deletion: https://cedarwavetechnologies.com/roofmates-delete-account.html.

Effective date: 10 October 2026 at 04:35:00 UTC. Version: 8 October 2026.

This policy explains how Roofmates handles information through its mobile apps, websites and related services. Roofmates supports shared living, including household membership, shared expenses and settlements, invitations, Events, Pools, participant-scoped conversations and selected household tools. The optional Plus offer adds routines, reminders, monthly check-ins and reusable handovers. The launch market is the United States; the locations where providers process data are a separate matter.

P01 Information we collect

We collect information you provide, information generated when you use Roofmates, and information supplied by the service providers used for the feature. The precise scope depends on the feature and its permissions. The categories below describe the adult account and online household service. Child-profile information is stored only on the family’s devices under P09 and is excluded from these online collection categories.

  • Account and authentication: name, email, authentication-provider subject and Roofmates user/participant identifiers, used to create, secure, recover and manage accounts.
  • Household and residency: household and room names, membership, role, residency and move-in/move-out context, profile image references and self-entered address or area details, used for household coordination and access control.
  • Manual safety contacts: contact labels, phone numbers, notes and service region entered by a user. These can concern another person. Manual entry is distinct from access to a device address book.
  • Finder and place preferences: city, neighborhood, desired areas, moving preferences and self-entered comparison answers. A desired search area is distinct from a current home address or device-derived location.
  • Finder listings, where offered: if you choose to publish a listing, we collect its title, city and neighborhood, rent and utility estimates, availability date, room description, amenities, preferences, and related household and publisher identifiers. We use this information to display and manage the listing. Published listings may be discovered by other registered Roofmates users according to your sharing choice, blocking rules and the publisher’s active household membership. This listing audience is separate from the audience for household financial records.
  • Financial and purchase records: merchant/title/date, amount and currency, payer and shares, obligations, confirmations, settlement/dispute status and audit history, used to maintain the shared ledger and verify purchases. Manual settlement records do not themselves move money.
  • User content: scoped text messages, Event/Pool details, guest references, chores, groceries, maintenance/safety/task details, notes and explicitly selected receipt/profile media where enabled. Chat and Maintenance attachment uploads are not currently offered.
  • Plus content: routine details, assignments, reminder preferences, completion/command history, monthly check-in agendas and notes, handover templates and instance records, and explicitly attached private handover photos. Paying for Plus does not widen content audiences.
  • Invitations and guests: guest display names, invitation/session credential digests, expiry, redemption and revocation state, used for limited secure access.
  • Device and notifications: installation/platform/app/OS details, locale/time zone, encrypted push tokens and permission/delivery status, used for consented notifications and delivery diagnostics.
  • Security and diagnostics: request/IP-related security signals, technical errors, bounded logs and audit events; optional crash and diagnostic information when configured. These online records must exclude child-profile information and identifiers used to track a child.
  • Native purchase evidence: product, store transaction/reference digests, verification and entitlement status, subscription lifecycle, refund/reversal/dispute events, used to administer paid access.
  • Support and moderation: correspondence and attachments you send, reports, blocks, content/evidence references, review/appeal notices and access/audit records, used to respond to requests and enforce safety rules.
  • Policy consent: accepted version, disclosure hash where applicable, action, account binding, timestamps and related evidence, used to demonstrate the scope and timing of consent and withdrawal.
  • Information others provide about you: other users may enter your name, phone number or other details about you — for example, in a shared expense, a safety contact, a guest reference or a note. If you are not a Roofmates user, see P08.
  • Coverage assignment records: which household a subscription or Permanent Purchase currently covers, and when that coverage moved or ended, used to administer paid access without granting record access.

Firebase Authentication may process an email address, password or Apple/Google provider credential, user-agent and IP address for authentication and abuse prevention. Roofmates does not receive passwords used with Apple or Google sign-in.

If an optional bank-linking feature is enabled, we will describe before collection the specific information involved, who receives it, why, how long it is kept and how to withdraw consent. Depending on the integration, this can include adult account-holder, routing and account numbers held in encrypted short-lived storage, bank-link metadata and provider responses. A dependent child profile cannot independently link a bank account.

Receipt images you choose to upload are sent to Roofmates and private storage. Scanning, where offered, can involve a configured processing provider. Public support and deletion webpages provide email request links. These public pages currently have no submitted webform or client-analytics feature. Necessary session cookies or temporary browser state may be used by an authentication provider when web sign-in is offered. Hosting providers may process connection information such as IP address and user-agent to deliver and secure the pages. Web account-deletion sign-in, where offered, uses Firebase Authentication to verify account control; its authentication processing is described above. Material changes to these practices are disclosed through the notice process in this policy.

P02 Information we do not collect for advertising

Roofmates does not sell personal information, serve third-party behavioral ads, or track people across other companies' apps and websites for advertising.

Do Not Track: Roofmates does not track your activity across other companies' apps and websites over time and does not allow third parties to do so through Roofmates, so it does not respond differently to browser Do Not Track signals.

Roofmates does not import your device address book. Users can manually enter safety-contact phone numbers and notes. Likewise, not using device location is different from having no location-related data: household addresses, areas and Finder preferences are described in P01.

Free-form fields may contain personal or sensitive information that a user chooses to enter. Avoid adding information unnecessary for the selected feature. Optional bank-linking features, if offered, are described separately in P01.

P03 Product improvement new features and AI

We may use service records and diagnostics to operate, secure, debug and improve the feature that produced them. We may use statistics that are aggregated or de-identified before analysis to evaluate reliability, understand existing features and plan new features. We do not attempt to re-identify that data.

Roofmates does not use identifiable private messages, receipt images, household information, financial records, support correspondence or other user content to train, fine-tune or evaluate general-purpose or Roofmates-specific AI models by default.

If we propose a future use of identifiable or user-provided content for model training, fine-tuning, evaluation or a materially different AI purpose, we will provide a separate, specific, default-off choice before that use begins. The choice will describe the data, purpose, model or provider, retention, withdrawal effect and any limits on removing information already incorporated into a trained artifact. Changing these Terms or this policy alone does not authorize that use.

P04 How information is shared

We disclose information only as needed for these purposes:

  • Selected participants. Content and financial records are visible to the household, Event, Pool, transaction or participant audience allowed by the feature. Administrator or Plus status does not override a participant-only visibility rule. Finder listings you choose to publish may be discovered by other registered Roofmates users as described in P01.
  • Service providers. Providers may process information on our behalf for authentication (Firebase/Google), infrastructure and storage (Cloudflare), diagnostics (Sentry when configured), push delivery (Apple and Google), customer support (Google Workspace), and payment verification if a paid feature is enabled. Their processing is limited by the applicable service, contract and instructions.
  • Legal and safety. We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law or lawful process, protect users or the public, investigate abuse or fraud, or establish, exercise or defend legal claims.
  • Business transfer. Information may transfer as part of a merger, financing, acquisition, reorganization or sale, subject to applicable law. We will require the recipient to continue protecting transferred personal information in accordance with the commitments applicable to it under this Privacy Policy. A business transfer alone does not authorize materially different uses of the information or override P03's separate, specific, default-off agreement requirement.

P05 Retention

We retain identifiable information only for a defined service, security, dispute-handling or legal purpose and for a period justified for that purpose. Account deletion and content deletion can require earlier removal than an ordinary maximum retention schedule. A label such as Deleted user does not make retained identifiers or related evidence anonymous.

We retain adult information only for the purposes described in this policy and remove or minimize it when those purposes end. Account and profile information is kept while the account is active, then handled under P06 and the account-deletion notice. Associated content is not retained merely because another participant saw it. The specific retention criteria below govern lawful exceptions; they do not permit retaining an entire deleted account or unrelated content. Child-profile information has no Roofmates server retention period because it is not collected or retained on our servers; P09 covers family-device storage.

  • Purchase evidence: minimal transaction references, purchase channel, product, charge date, amount and currency, refund or reversal status, and verification records may be kept while needed to administer a valid purchase, verify recovery, resolve an actual dispute or fraud issue, or meet an applicable accounting, tax or legal duty. When no such purpose remains, the evidence is removed or minimized. This exception does not retain deleted household content or payment credentials.
  • Support and security: adult support correspondence is kept for case resolution and necessary follow-up or a documented legal need. Diagnostic, security and restricted moderation records are kept only while needed for the specific reliability, safety, dispute or legal purpose that produced them, with access restricted and periodic removal or minimization. A possible future use alone does not justify indefinite retention.
  • Purchase notices after deletion: with separate permission, we may keep minimal contact information solely for notices and recovery relating to the buyer’s valid purchase. The buyer may withdraw that permission through support. The optional contact is removed when permission is withdrawn or the purchase and any related open claim no longer require it, unless a separate legal duty requires a specified record. It does not preserve the deleted account.
  • Backups and lawful holds: deleted data awaiting backup rotation is unavailable for normal use and is removed through that cycle; if a backup is restored, deletion must be reapplied before normal use. A lawful hold is limited to the required information and purpose and is released when that duty ends. Independent payment-provider records follow that provider’s applicable obligations.

If we collect consent evidence for an adult bank-linking feature, we retain the identified evidence for seven calendar years after the later of the last consent event for that purpose or account closure. We then erase the complete record, subject to a specifically documented lawful preservation requirement. The evidence is used only to establish permission, withdrawal or deletion and for narrowly related legal, fraud or dispute inquiries. It cannot restore bank access and is not used for analytics, advertising or AI training.

These adult online-retention rules do not authorize collecting or retaining child-profile information on Roofmates servers. Children’s local storage, parental control and deletion are governed by P09.

P06 Account deletion

You can request account deletion using the in-app account-deletion control or https://cedarwavetechnologies.com/roofmates-delete-account.html. Open shared obligations do not prevent deletion. We may verify that you control the account using a safe account/provider verification step; do not email a password or authentication code.

The deletion flow removes account access, deactivates memberships, retires device installations and notification tokens, scrubs relevant direct profile fields, requests authentication-provider deletion, revokes access to private media, and queues applicable object cleanup.

Deletion addresses user-generated content associated with your account, including shared photos and text, as well as account identifiers. We keep specific information only under the limited exceptions and retention criteria in P05 and D03, and we tell you what is kept, why and for how long. We do not keep every shared record merely because other household members relied on it. Retained consent records and some audit references remain personal information even after profile details are removed.

Before you confirm deletion, we explain what will be deleted, what is kept, the expected completion timing, how providers and backups are handled, and how subscription billing is affected (D04 and D05).

P07 Security

We use safeguards designed to protect account and household information, including access controls and measures appropriate to the data processed. No system can guarantee absolute security.

If a security incident affects your personal information, we will provide notices to affected people, regulators and other recipients as required by applicable law, using the required timing, methods and information.

P08 Your choices and rights

Depending on where a user lives, they may have rights to access, correct, delete, port, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent, appeal a request decision, or opt out of certain sale, sharing, profiling or automated decision uses. Roofmates does not currently sell personal information or use it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Use in-app controls or email roofmates-support@cedarwavetechnologies.com. We may verify identity and will explain if law or another person's rights require us to retain or withhold specific information. Notification permission can be changed in device settings. Transactional records may remain visible inside Roofmates even when push notifications are disabled.

Parents and legal guardians manage device-only child-profile information through the local parent controls in P09. A parent or legal guardian may contact support on a child’s behalf for instructions without sending the child’s information; we cannot retrieve a profile that was never stored on our servers.

If you are not a Roofmates user and believe someone entered information about you, you can contact roofmates-support@cedarwavetechnologies.com to ask about it. We will explain what we can do, including any limits that the law or another person's rights require.

P09 Children and parent linked profiles

Independent Roofmates accounts, purchases and adult guest access are available only to people at least 18 years old who are legally able to enter a binding contract. A parent or legal guardian may create a dependent child profile through the parent-managed feature, where available. This dependent child profile stays under the adult’s supervision and does not create an independent cloud account, contract, subscription or bank account for the child.

Chores allowances and household money

A dependent child profile may support locally assigned chores, completion tracking, allowances and chore rewards. Only the parent or legal guardian may enable and manage it. The child’s access is limited to the local information and controls the parent makes available; it does not permit online messaging, online posting, public listings or access to adult financial records.

An allowance or reward recorded in a child profile is a local record, not a bank balance or confirmation that money moved. Money-transfer and withdrawal services are not currently offered. If a household money-sharing feature is offered later, a parent or legal guardian must initiate and authorize any payment through an eligible provider under the separately disclosed terms. A child cannot independently authorize a payment, purchase, subscription or withdrawal. Any proposed payment flow that would transmit child-profile information requires a separate privacy notice and any legally required parental consent before that flow can be enabled.

Children’s data storage and retention

Roofmates does not collect or retain child-profile data on its servers. The child’s local profile, its link to the parent, any name or nickname, chore assignments, completion history, allowance records and reward amounts remain only on the family’s devices. They are not uploaded to Roofmates or its service providers for any purpose, including synchronization, storage, analytics, advertising, AI training or support. Child-profile data and child-specific identifiers are excluded from online diagnostics, server logs, push-notification payloads and backups operated by Roofmates.

The parent’s adult account and its online records remain subject to P01–P08. Creating a local child profile does not authorize us to create a server-side record about the child. Do not copy a child’s personal information from the profile into an online household record, payment memo, support attachment or other shared field. The device-only restriction also means Roofmates does not offer server-based synchronization, cloud restoration or remote recovery of child-profile information.

Parental control and local deletion

The parent or legal guardian can review, correct or delete the child profile and its local information using the parent controls on the device. Local information remains available while the parent keeps the profile and removes it through those controls when no longer needed. Deleting the profile removes its local records; no copy is retained on Roofmates servers. Turning off child access does not itself erase local records, so use the deletion control when you want them removed.

If you use a device backup or export outside Roofmates, copies follow that separate service’s settings and policies and must be removed through it. Remove child profiles using local parent controls on each family device before deleting the parent account. Deletion started on the web or another device does not itself erase local profiles on other family devices or their separately managed backups; use those local controls and the backup service to remove the copies. Roofmates has no child-profile server copy or index to restore or remotely erase. Local deletion does not reverse an adult payment or erase records a payment provider must separately retain.

Children under thirteen and accidental submissions

The same device-only limits apply to children under 13. A parent-linked profile is not permission to collect a child’s personal information online. We will not enable any change that collects, uses or discloses such information online until the required notices, parental controls and any legally required verifiable parental consent are in place. We do not sell child-profile information or use it for advertising or AI training.

If you believe a child has created an independent account or that child-profile information has been sent to Roofmates, contact roofmates-support@cedarwavetechnologies.com without sending the child’s personal information in the first message. We will investigate, stop unauthorized collection or access, and delete information received in error promptly, except for a specific preservation obligation imposed by law. Any legally required preservation is limited to that obligation, restricted from other use, and ends when the obligation ends.

P10 International processing

Roofmates is launching for US customers first. Information may be processed by infrastructure and service providers outside the customer's state or country. US-only sales do not establish US-only storage or processing.

Our infrastructure and service providers may operate in the United States and other countries. Processing locations depend on the provider and configured service; we do not promise that all information stays in the United States. Any applicable cross-border safeguards remain our responsibility for processing on our behalf. Provider privacy information is available from Google/Firebase at https://policies.google.com/privacy, Cloudflare at https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/, and Sentry at https://sentry.io/privacy/. Contact support about our processing and applicable privacy requests.

P11 Changes

We may update this policy as the Service or law changes. We will post the new effective date and provide additional notice when required. A material change does not retroactively authorize a materially different use without any notice or consent required by law.

We notify users of material changes through the notice channels described in T20, which may include an in-app notice or email, and we show the new effective date at the top of this policy.
