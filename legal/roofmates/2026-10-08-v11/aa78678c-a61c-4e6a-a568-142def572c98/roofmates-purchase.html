Purchase cancellation and refund policy

Effective 10 October 2026 at 04:35:00 UTC. This is an archived, versioned policy document.

This policy supplements the Terms for Roofmates paid offers.

B01 Free core

You can participate in Roofmates' free core without buying Plus. Basic tools, records and safety controls remain available under the normal account and household permissions. Buying Plus does not give anyone access to another participant's private information. Money transfer and withdrawal services are not currently offered. If they are offered later, transfer and withdrawal access will be part of Free, will not require Plus, and will be subject to separate terms, fees and disclosures shown before you can use them.

B02 Buyer-owned household Plus subscriptions

The selected US subscription prices are US$5.99 per month or US$49.99 per year. The selected billing period, currency, total price and applicable taxes are shown before payment. Household Plus includes recurring routines, shared reminders, monthly check-ins and reusable move-in/move-out handovers for all current authorized members of the covered household. This Household Plus offer has no separate personal Premium bundle; rights from existing purchases remain protected under B08.

A monthly or annual subscription follows its buyer and can cover one selected household at a time in which the buyer is a current authorized member. The buyer may move coverage to another eligible household or keep the purchase without selecting a household. There is no commercial limit on how often coverage may move; the one-household rule and fraud and abuse safeguards still apply. Moving coverage does not move household records or grant access to records the buyer is no longer authorized to see. Another member is never silently made responsible for payment.

If the buyer leaves or is removed from the covered household, this purchase’s coverage of that household ends. The buyer keeps the verified purchase and may cover another eligible household. The former household loses this purchase’s coverage when it ends or moves, unless another valid entitlement provides coverage. For subscriptions, billing continues until the buyer cancels through the original purchase channel.

Leaving, being removed from or permanently closing a household does not cancel subscription billing. The payer cancels separately through the original purchase channel. If your subscription is not currently covering any household, billing still continues until you cancel. Paying alone never grants access to household records.

Before you leave or close a household, we show you that billing continues until you cancel, how to cancel through your purchase channel, which household your subscription currently covers and when your current paid period ends. If someone else removes you from a household that your subscription covers, we tell you the same information.

When this subscription's coverage of a household ends or moves away, we tell the household's current members that Plus coverage has ended, without sharing the payer's payment details.

Subscriptions renew automatically for the selected period until canceled through the purchase provider, except the service-closure renewal stop in B10. Any trial, promotion, renewal price or price change is disclosed before it applies with required notice/consent. No trial is promised.

Any billing grace period is set by your purchase channel; Plus continues during a grace period only while the channel reports that access continues. No grace period of a particular length is promised.

B03 Cancellation expiry and household closure

Ordinary cancellation stops future renewal; Plus continues through the verified entitlement end date shown by the purchase provider and any applicable provider-granted grace. Uninstalling Roofmates, signing out, household exit, or household closure does not automatically cancel store billing. The payer manages ordinary cancellation through the purchase channel. Full account deletion follows D05: we stop Google Play and direct-billing renewals and future charges, and guide standard Apple cancellation. We show the purchase channel, any outstanding cancellation step and the household being funded. You may delete your account immediately without waiting for subscription expiry.

Permanently closing a household detaches its coverage and does not automatically refund the subscription. The buyer keeps the verified subscription entitlement for the remaining paid period and may cover another eligible household under B02. Ordinary refund requests remain available under B11. A permanent buyer keeps their verified buyer-owned entitlement under B04-B08; household closure or disuse does not forfeit it.

When coverage ends or moves, paid automations stop in a household that has no other active entitlement. Ending paid coverage does not itself delete existing records or put them behind a new payment requirement. Access remains subject to membership permissions, user deletion, lawful retention and the account-deletion policy. Closing a household does not make its records visible to anyone who was not already authorized to see them, and does not change existing permissions.

On verified billing hold or expiry, access reverts to Free as the purchase channel requires. A failed payment attempt does not by itself create a charge, and Plus resumes only after a payment succeeds, as your purchase channel reports it. Cancellation alone does not automatically create a refund. Permanent loss of the purchased subscription service follows B10-B11, distinct from a user's household closure.

B04 Permanent Purchase price and ownership

The US price is US$249.99, paid once, with no recurring charge for this purchase. The total price, currency and applicable taxes will be shown before payment. The purchase belongs to the buyer and covers one Roofmates household they select at a time. All current authorized members of that household can use its included Plus features without buying separate memberships. Joining a household does not give another member ownership of the purchase.

B05 Permanent Purchase features

The offer currently includes recurring household routines, shared reminders, monthly check-ins and reusable move-in/move-out handovers. The one-time purchase has no scheduled expiry or recurring charge and follows its buyer under B06-B07. Features and workflows may evolve under T09; a Permanent Purchase is not a promise that every feature, design or supported device will remain unchanged forever. The product and included functionality are shown before payment.

Separately offered usage-heavy AI services or paid third-party integrations are not automatically included. Their scope and any additional charge are shown before the buyer opts in. A service change does not automatically enroll the buyer in a new subscription or authorize another charge.

Stored records remain subject to authorization, privacy, deletion and lawful retention rules, and the closure/export provisions in B10. Refunds follow B10-B11. This offer adds no voluntary Permanent Purchase feature-change refund guarantee beyond those express rules. Applicable law, purchase-provider rights and the historical purchase rights in B08 remain unaffected.

B06 Moving permanent coverage between households

The Permanent Purchase belongs to its original buyer. It may cover one selected household at a time in which the buyer is a current authorized member. The buyer may move coverage to another eligible household or hold the entitlement without selecting a household. There is no commercial limit on how often coverage may move; the one-household rule and fraud and abuse safeguards still apply. If the buyer leaves or is removed from the covered household, its coverage from this purchase ends, and the buyer keeps the entitlement. Moving coverage does not move household records. Changing a household administrator does not transfer ownership.

The former household's paid automations stop when it no longer has another active entitlement. Ending paid coverage does not itself delete existing records or put them behind a new payment requirement. Access remains subject to membership permissions, user deletion, lawful retention limits and the account-deletion policy. Moving coverage does not grant former members access to household records. When permanent coverage ends or moves, we tell the household’s current members that this coverage has ended, without sharing the buyer’s payment details.

B07 Ownership transfer recovery and account deletion

The purchase cannot be sold, gifted after purchase or transferred to another person, except where applicable law requires otherwise. Verified recovery into a replacement Roofmates account controlled by the same buyer is permitted and does not create a second entitlement. We will not require another purchase merely because you replaced a supported device or lost access to the original Roofmates account.

Deleting your account detaches household coverage and removes account and content data under the deletion policy, without forfeiting a verified paid entitlement. Later recovery restores purchase access only, never deleted records or former household membership. Before deletion, we explain available original-buyer verification and any securely saved recovery credential needed for later recovery. Choosing not to set up recovery does not prevent deletion. We lawfully keep only the minimal purchase evidence needed under P05, not the whole account. Recovery requires current purchase verification plus control by the same original buyer through an available original-provider, securely saved credential or verified-support route. A public transaction identifier or reused email address alone does not prove ownership. The original purchase date and applicable refund periods remain unchanged.

A Permanent Purchase is designed to work as one entitlement for the same buyer across supported Roofmates clients, subject to law and technical support. The supported clients and restore routes will be shown at checkout. This does not promise support for all platforms or future operating systems, or transfer the underlying transaction between stores. Roofmates household coverage is not a claim of Apple Family Sharing or Google family-purchase sharing.

B08 Existing purchases

The new offer does not reduce rights attached to existing purchases. Existing individual Lifetime licenses and group purchases of individual licenses retain their existing rights; they are not automatically converted to the household offer. We retain and honor the historical terms applicable to each purchase.

B09 Changes of plan and duplicate payments

If a household already has current Plus coverage, we will not offer it a second new household subscription. A member joining with an existing purchase keeps that purchase; it may remain unassigned or cover another eligible household under B02 or B06. Additional purchases do not increase the household’s included benefits. Authorized viewers can see the selected household coverage source and when it ends, without the payer’s financial details. To change from monthly to annual, use the purchase channel’s supported plan-change option; its price and effective date are shown before confirmation.

When members of the same household have separate active subscriptions, we notify each affected buyer that the household already has Plus and another subscription is not required for that household’s included benefits. Each buyer sees their own billing status and cancellation route, without another buyer’s payment details. Each buyer decides whether to keep or cancel their subscription through the original purchase channel. We do not automatically cancel, pause, merge or refund either subscription solely because the household already has coverage. Unless canceled through the purchase channel, each subscription continues under its own billing terms.

Permanent Purchase does not cancel a store subscription. To avoid paying twice, end the existing subscription's renewal and wait until coverage expires before purchasing Permanent Purchase. No automatic credit or prorated refund is promised for an ordinary voluntary change. Mandatory rights and purchase-provider refund rights remain available. Ordinary refund requests under B11 also remain available.

We will not charge you now for coverage that would start later without telling you before you pay.

If a Roofmates checkout or entitlement error causes a confirmed erroneous duplicate charge, we correct it in full, even if the ordinary refund-request window has passed. We refund the actual erroneous duplicate amount, less any refund already issued for that same error; the valid original charge remains in place. We coordinate with the purchase channel to avoid double reimbursement. This is not a promise that a store refunds every intentional overlap. This correction is an owed refund under B11. Contact roofmates-support@cedarwavetechnologies.com to report the error; do not send payment credentials.

The separate 30-day ordinary voluntary-refund request rules are in B11. They do not create an automatic plan-change credit or limit correction of a confirmed duplicate error. We verify reported duplicate charges before correcting them.

Private sharing of subscription costs

Adult household members may voluntarily agree to share the cost privately, including a 50/50 split for an agreed period. The billing owner remains responsible for the full subscription charge. Members settle contributions outside Roofmates; Roofmates does not collect or enforce them. A contribution does not transfer purchase ownership or expand record access. Members handle repayments and adjustments after a refund under their private agreement; Roofmates refunds follow B11.

If members have separate subscriptions, each buyer manages cancellation through the original purchase channel. Private cost sharing does not merge, pause or cancel either purchase, or make another member the billing owner. Coverage and cancellation follow B02-B03.

B10 Permanent benefits and service closure

Permanent means the purchase has no scheduled expiry or renewal fee. Online functions depend on Roofmates operating and features may evolve under T09 and B05. The purchase does not promise operation forever or support for every future device or operating-system version. The specific covered-closure protection below is separate from ordinary feature changes and the ordinary refund window.

Planned closure notice and export

If we decide to close the Roofmates service permanently, we will give at least 90 calendar days' advance notice by email to your current account email and in the app. During that period, current authorized users may access and export the existing records they are entitled to see without buying or renewing Plus. The notice will state the planned closure date, the final access and export date if it is different, export instructions, the last day to request a subscription closure refund under B11 counted from the planned closure date, how to request any Permanent Purchase closure protection that applies, and the applicable purchase remedy. If the covered service actually closes on a different date, the B11 request period runs from the actual closure date and we will tell you the revised last day.

This access does not restore a former member's access, override another person's privacy rights, or recreate records already lawfully deleted. A legal prohibition or an urgent security issue may require shorter notice or restricted access; we will explain the restriction and provide as much lawful notice and safe access as reasonably possible. Those circumstances do not by themselves eliminate your purchase remedy or legal rights.

Exports will provide structured data in a common machine-readable format such as CSV or JSON, readable summaries where needed, authorized original attachments in an archive and a list of omitted or previously deleted items. Export will not require a new third-party account or an export charge. If an export outage caused by Roofmates prevents export, we will extend the export deadline by the length of that outage where safe and lawful. Exports are limited to the records each member is authorized to see. Roofmates server exports do not contain device-only child-profile records, which Roofmates does not hold; local parent controls and separately managed exports are described in P09.

When we announce a planned closure, new sales and subscription renewals stop while promised notice-period access continues. The same notice and export provisions apply, to the extent safe and lawful, to permanent loss of a purchased supported platform or region with no usable equivalent. A usable equivalent preserves the applicable purchased service that the buyer can actually access without another purchase or subscription charge. Those covered events follow B11 for subscriptions and the purchase-to-closure limit below for Permanent Purchases. With separate permission under P05, minimal purchase contact information may be used for notices after account deletion; without it, public closure notices at https://cedarwavetechnologies.com/roofmates-support.html and available purchase-provider routes remain accessible. No whole deleted account is kept for notice delivery.

Permanent purchase closure protection

If a covered closure, as described in this section, occurs within 90 calendar days of your Permanent Purchase, the additional contractual closure refund is the full actual purchase payment, less refunds already issued for that same purchase to avoid double reimbursement. If covered closure occurs after that purchase-to-closure protection period, this rule provides no additional contractual closure refund. Mandatory legal rights, applicable purchase-provider rights and protected historical purchase terms remain unaffected. No declining formula applies, and there is no unlimited full-price refund promise.

The 90-day purchase-to-closure period is separate from the claim deadline. Covered closure includes the service closing permanently or permanent loss of the purchased supported platform or region with no usable equivalent, as described above. You must request a qualifying Permanent Purchase closure refund within 90 calendar days after actual covered closure at roofmates-support@cedarwavetechnologies.com. Purchase, closure and claim dates use UTC, with the event date as day zero and the final numbered day included through 23:59:59 UTC. Announcement and the export-access endpoint do not start the claim clock.

How this protection relates to closure notice: the 90-day period runs from your purchase to the date the covered service actually closes, not to the date a closure is announced. Because we announce a planned closure at least 90 calendar days in advance, a planned closure will generally occur more than 90 days after any purchase made before the announcement, so this protection will generally not apply to those purchases. It mainly applies if Roofmates closes with less than 90 days' notice — for example, where a legal prohibition or an urgent security issue requires shorter notice — or in an unplanned closure. This explanation does not change the rule above. Ordinary refund requests (B11) and your mandatory, purchase-channel and protected historical rights are separate.

Ordinary feature additions, changes or redesigns do not by themselves constitute covered service closure. A Permanent Purchase has no scheduled expiry; the separate refund rules in B10-B11 and applicable mandatory, provider and historical rights remain in effect.

B11 Refunds and consumer rights

Subscription closure refund

Permanent loss of the purchased subscription service qualifies, including loss of a platform or region with no usable equivalent. If that covered closure occurs during a paid subscription period, you may request a closure refund. For a monthly subscription, the refund is the full amount actually paid for the active monthly period. For an annual subscription, the refund is the actual annual price paid multiplied by (12 minus the number of completed subscription months in that annual period), divided by 12. An incomplete current subscription month is refunded in full as one twelfth of the annual price paid. For example, if closure occurs five and a half subscription months into the annual period, the refund is seven twelfths of that annual payment. We do not recalculate completed annual-plan months at the standalone monthly subscription price.

We deduct refunds already issued for the same refundable coverage so it is not reimbursed twice. Eligibility does not depend on whether or how often you used the app. This contractual closure refund covers only the subscription period active when the closure described above occurs. Earlier completed periods are not refundable under this rule, without limiting mandatory legal rights, purchase-provider rights or protected historical terms. You must request this contractual subscription closure refund within 90 calendar days after the covered service actually closes.

The request clock starts at actual covered closure, not announcement or the export-access endpoint. It is separate from B10's advance closure notice and permanent purchase-to-closure protection. Covered unplanned closure is not excluded merely because an advance notice was impossible. Subscription renewals stop at the announcement while promised notice-period access is preserved. B10's closure notice states the planned closure date and the resulting last day to request; if the covered service actually closes on a different date, the 90 days run from the actual closure date.

Subscription months are counted from the purchase or renewal anniversary within the active annual period, not fixed 30-day blocks. Calculations and contractual deadlines use UTC. An anniversary day missing from a month is moved to that month’s last day, using the original period start as the anchor. The event date is day zero and the final numbered deadline day is included through 23:59:59 UTC. The final refund is rounded once to the currency’s smallest unit; tax adjustments are reconciled with the original charge, applicable law and purchase-channel handling.

Ordinary voluntary refund requests

You may request an ordinary voluntary refund within 30 calendar days after paid access for each purchase or renewal activates. For a renewal, the clock starts when that renewal’s paid coverage begins, not at your first-ever activation. If approved, the refund is the full amount actually paid for that purchase or renewal, less any refund already issued for the same charge. Activation means the verified start of paid purchase access, even when no household has been selected. Selecting or changing a household later does not restart the window.

For purchases Roofmates is permitted to refund, we approve a request made within this window without requiring a reason, except for verified fraud. Amounts already refunded for the same charge are not refunded again. Apple handles its own refund decisions as described below.

A refund form, where available, may offer reasons such as Other and Accidentally purchased. Paid features removed may be offered for a monthly subscription and applies only to its most recent paid monthly charge within the ordinary 30-day request window, less any refund already issued for that charge. It does not refund earlier months or restart the window. Annual subscribers may cancel renewal and keep paid access until the paid year ends; feature changes create no special annual refund. The ordinary 30-day window rules and the other express, legal and purchase-provider refund rights in this policy remain separate.

How a request is handled depends on where you paid:

  • Apple App Store purchases: Apple handles refund requests for App Store purchases, and the decision is Apple’s. Request a refund at https://reportaproblem.apple.com/ or see https://support.apple.com/118223. We may support your request.
  • Google Play purchases: you may send your request to roofmates-support@cedarwavetechnologies.com within the 30-day window. If we approve it, we issue the refund through Google Play where Google Play allows it. Google Play’s own refund options remain available: https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637.
  • Direct Roofmates checkout: if offered, its refund-request route will be shown at checkout.

This ordinary process does not replace closure refunds, the Permanent Purchase closure protection or correction of confirmed duplicate errors. If a store declines an ordinary voluntary refund, Roofmates may try to assist or consider a feasible alternative, but does not guarantee an additional company refund or a particular outcome. That limitation does not discharge a refund Roofmates already owes under this policy (see "Owed refunds and corrections" below).

Once a full ordinary refund is confirmed, paid access funded by that charge ends. Other valid purchases and their household coverage continue. Existing records remain accessible under normal authorization, deletion and lawful retention rules; the refund does not itself delete or newly paywall them.

Serious or repeated violations and appeals

Termination for a serious or repeated violation does not itself create an additional voluntary refund entitlement. You may appeal under T19/C03. Relevant information used in the termination decision may be used in that appeal, subject to applicable law, privacy duties, data minimization, authorized access and applicable retention rules. This does not authorize indefinite evidence retention, unrelated use or third-party disclosure, or eliminate any refund already owed or mandatory, store/provider or protected historical rights.

Owed refunds and corrections

Roofmates owes the expressly promised qualifying subscription closure refund, Permanent Purchase covered-closure refund and confirmed duplicate-charge correction when their conditions are met. An owed refund does not depend on a store approving an ordinary voluntary request. If the original purchase channel cannot issue it, we pay the verified original buyer by another lawful method agreed with that buyer. We issue payment within 60 calendar days after receiving a complete, verifiable claim, or sooner where required. We request only necessary information to verify the charge, ownership and prior refunds through a secure process; do not send payment credentials by ordinary email. Mandatory legal, purchase-provider and protected historical rights remain unaffected.

Channels reversals and mandatory rights

Use the applicable purchase-channel route and consumer law. Contact roofmates-support@cedarwavetechnologies.com for help identifying the billing channel. Roofmates does not promise a store will grant every request. A referral to support is not by itself payment of an owed refund.

A verified refund, reversal or purchase dispute may affect only its associated paid access as permitted by law and the purchase channel. A temporary restriction during a documented review preserves other valid purchases. We explain the affected purchase and available verification or appeal route where safe and lawful. Final revocation follows verified refund, reversal, invalidation or fraudulent-ownership evidence; mistakes are corrected and valid access restored without recreating deleted content. Account enforcement separately follows T19. Ending paid coverage does not itself delete or paywall records; normal authorization, deletion and lawful retention govern access.

The Permanent Purchase closure protection is in B10. Mandatory legal, applicable purchase-provider and protected historical rights remain unaffected throughout this policy.

B12 Information shown before purchase

Before any purchase, we show you: the product and its included benefits; whether it is one-time or recurring; the price, currency and total including tax; for subscriptions, the renewal interval and renewal price, with the amount billed for each period as the most prominent price (an annual plan is not presented mainly as a monthly equivalent); any trial or promotion and what it converts to; how to cancel; this refund policy; the provider of the purchase; and links to these policies. For a Permanent Purchase, we also show the scope and limit of the closure protection in B10, including that it applies only if a covered closure occurs within 90 days of purchase.

We ask for your affirmative consent to the purchase and, for subscriptions, to recurring charges, and we keep evidence of the exact offer you accepted. Your account or settings area includes a link to manage or cancel subscriptions through your purchase channel.

Official subscription-management help: Apple, https://apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions/; Google Play, https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/7018481. Always use the channel through which you purchased.
