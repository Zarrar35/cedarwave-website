Account deletion notice

Effective 10 October 2026 at 04:35:00 UTC. This is an archived, versioned policy document.

D01 Request deletion

You can request deletion of your Roofmates account in the app using Delete account, or through https://cedarwavetechnologies.com/roofmates-delete-account.html. Open shared obligations do not prevent deletion. Deleting your account in the app does not require contacting support.

Without the app, you can request deletion by emailing roofmates-support@cedarwavetechnologies.com from the account-associated email, including that email address and the words Delete my Roofmates account. Do not include a password, authentication code, payment details or identity document in the first message. We may verify account control through a safe account/provider step.

If you cannot email us from your account’s email address, including when you used Sign in with Apple and chose to hide your email, contact roofmates-support@cedarwavetechnologies.com for a safe account-control verification route. Do not send passwords or authentication codes. An adult guest with no account-associated email should contact support without sending a guest token or private invitation link. We use a secure check of guest-session control or another minimal record demonstrating control of that guest identity. Verification does not disclose another participant’s private records or require an email address that was never collected.

D02 What deletion covers

Deletion removes account access and addresses associated personal information and user-generated content. The deletion process deactivates memberships, retires device and push access, removes relevant direct profile data, requests deletion from the authentication provider and queues removal of applicable private media. This covers associated messages, receipts, handover photos, notes, listings, Events and Pools, routines and check-ins, subject only to the specific lawful exceptions in D03. Shared items are not kept merely because another participant saw them. Device-only child profiles are removed locally as described in P09; there is no Roofmates server copy to retain or restore.

D03 Specifically retained information

The limited adult purchase, consent, support, security and lawful-hold exceptions and their retention criteria are described in P05 and B07. Each retained record must serve its specified purpose, have restricted access and be removed or minimized when that purpose and any applicable legal duty end. A Deleted user label does not make an account reference, financial record, consent or audit reference anonymous. These adult exceptions do not create server retention for child-profile data; P09’s device-only limits apply.

Information we are legally required to preserve — for example, material connected with a report the law requires us to make — is kept access-restricted and only for the period the law requires.

Adult bank-link consent evidence, if collected, remains identified and access-restricted for the seven-calendar-year period described in P05. It cannot restore bank access and does not authorize blanket retention of other user content or any child-profile data on Roofmates servers.

D04 Deletion timing backups and confirmation

Before you confirm deletion, we explain what will be deleted, specific information lawfully retained, the expected completion date and any remaining billing step. We confirm receipt and provide a protected status route that can remain available after account access ends. Keep that receipt secure; it is not a purchase-recovery credential. Receipt of a request or removal of account access does not mean every provider, content or storage step is complete. We notify you when deletion is complete. If a legally permitted delay is needed, we explain its reason and revised timing within the applicable notice deadline. Retained backup or legal records are identified by purpose, access restriction and expiry criteria. Unpaid household obligations do not delay deletion.

We begin processing a verified deletion request without unnecessary delay and provide the expected completion date. Provider cleanup and backup rotation may require separate steps; we explain a necessary delay and revised timing within applicable notice requirements. A completion notice identifies any specifically retained lawful exception. Backups are unavailable for normal use while awaiting rotation, and deletion must be reapplied if a backup is restored before normal use.

D05 Subscriptions and purchase recovery during deletion

Full account deletion stops future renewals and charges for Google Play and direct-billing subscriptions owned by the deleted account. Roofmates processes the stop for Google Play and direct billing and verifies the result with the provider. An outstanding or failed provider step remains unconfirmed in the deletion status. For standard Apple subscriptions, you must cancel through Apple. We show Apple subscription controls and warn when billing remains active or unconfirmed; opening those controls alone does not confirm cancellation. You may delete your account immediately without waiting for Apple cancellation or the paid period to end. Uninstalling Roofmates or signing out while the account remains does not stop the subscription.

Deleting an account detaches its household coverage and revokes account access without forfeiting a valid remaining paid entitlement. The same original buyer may recover verified purchase access into a replacement account while that entitlement remains valid, through an available verified recovery route. Recovery requires current purchase verification and proof of the original buyer; a public transaction identifier or reused email address alone is insufficient. Contact support if automatic verification cannot resolve the claim. Recovery restores paid access only, never deleted content or former household membership, and does not automatically restart renewal or reset the original purchase or refund dates. Permanent Purchase recovery follows B07 and closure protection follows B10. Deletion does not settle a private obligation, transfer purchase ownership or create an additional refund.

D06 Help and privacy requests

For deletion failures or privacy requests, contact roofmates-support@cedarwavetechnologies.com. Do not send credentials. We may verify account control or identity through a safe process and explain any lawful limits or applicable appeal rights. A parent or legal guardian may contact us on a child’s behalf for instructions. Requests to remove a child’s device-only profile must use the local parent controls described in P09 because Roofmates has no server copy to delete.
