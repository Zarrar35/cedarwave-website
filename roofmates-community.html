Community and safety policy

Effective 10 October 2026 at 04:35:00 UTC. This is an archived, versioned policy document.

C01 Content and conduct rules

Roofmates does not allow:

  • threats, violence, stalking, coercion, or encouragement of self-harm;
  • child sexual exploitation, grooming, trafficking, or sexual content involving a person under 18;
  • non-consensual intimate imagery or threats to share it;
  • hate, discrimination, targeted harassment, bullying, or impersonation;
  • doxxing, exposure of private credentials or precise private location, or unauthorized disclosure of another person's sensitive information;
  • scams, fraud, phishing, malware, or deceptive requests for money;
  • illegal, defamatory, or rights-infringing content, including copyright infringement;
  • spam or conduct designed to evade product, account, safety, or moderation controls.

Context, severity, repetition, credibility, target vulnerability, and prior enforcement inform the response. A report is a safety signal, not proof that the reported person committed a crime or civil wrong.

Adult content boundary

Pornographic or sexually explicit content is prohibited in all Roofmates spaces, including household-only posts, messages, profiles, listings, photos and attachments. This includes explicit sexual imagery, sexual activity and graphic sexual text intended for sexual gratification. Sexual exploitation, grooming, sexual content involving minors, non-consensual intimate imagery and sexual harassment are prohibited. Reports about abuse may describe the concern without uploading prohibited imagery. Neutral, non-graphic discussion of health, identity, safety or household boundaries is not prohibited solely because it mentions sex.

Access to an adult account or a dependent child profile never permits explicit content.

C02 Reporting and blocking

Use the in-app report control to report supported content or a user, including a guest, and the block control to block a user. For help or an appeal, contact roofmates-support@cedarwavetechnologies.com. A report is a safety signal, not a finding that someone committed a crime or civil wrong.

Blocking is private and limits what your account sees according to the feature. For direct one-to-one messaging, blocking stops new direct messages between the blocked identities; it does not by itself erase prior messages. It does not remove either person from a household, Event, Pool, shared ledger or legal relationship. It does not erase shared obligations or replace a separate administrator, payment-dispute or emergency process.

C03 Review and enforcement

Roofmates may investigate reports, warn users, conceal or remove content, and take other proportionate content-moderation action consistent with law and these policies. Restrictions on account access, including posting or messaging restrictions, suspension and termination, follow T19. Context, severity, repetition, credibility, vulnerability and prior enforcement may affect the response. Evidence access is limited to authorized personnel and applicable retention rules.

Where safe and lawful, we tell a user affected by an enforcement decision the applicable rule, the scope of the action and the appeal route, and we send reporting users a neutral confirmation without disclosing another person's confidential account information.

C04 Emergency and safety boundaries

Roofmates is not an emergency service and does not guarantee immediate human review. If someone is in immediate danger, contact the emergency service appropriate to your location. Household safety and maintenance features do not automatically contact emergency responders or establish that a property is safe.

C05 Copyright and unlawful material

Do not post content you lack the right to share. Report suspected copyright infringement through the report control or roofmates-support@cedarwavetechnologies.com. Identify the work, the content complained of and enough information to locate it, provide contact information and your authority to act, and include an electronic or physical signature and statements of good-faith belief and accuracy. We may request necessary clarification and apply the applicable removal, notice and challenge procedures. Use text identifiers rather than emailing prohibited or infringing images.

For an intimate image or video posted without consent, the depicted person or an authorized representative may send a written removal request to roofmates-support@cedarwavetechnologies.com, whether or not they have an account. Include a physical or electronic signature, enough information to identify and locate the content, a brief good-faith statement that it was posted without consent, and a way to contact the requester. Do not attach the image. Where the Take It Down Act applies, valid requests are handled as soon as possible and within 48 hours, including reasonable efforts to identify and remove known identical copies. We provide an identifying case reference and an outcome or necessary clarification.

Child exploitation, threats and other unlawful material are handled through restricted, lawful escalation and preservation procedures, including any legally required reports. Do not send suspected illegal images through ordinary support email. Use the report control or contact support without attaching the material.

C06 Shared audiences and paid access

These rules apply to content shared with even a limited household or selected participants. Private or limited-audience sharing does not exempt content from safety rules. Paying for Plus does not override permissions, reporting, blocking or enforcement. Safety controls remain part of the free core.
