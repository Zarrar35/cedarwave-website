Questions

What Roofmates
is — and is not.

Who is Roofmates for?

Adults coordinating a shared household — roommates, friends, co-living houses, and people setting up a new home together. Independent accounts and adult guests are for people age 18 or older. Parents can supervise device-only child profiles through separate guardian controls.

Does Roofmates hold or transfer money?

No. Roofmates is a coordination tool and shared ledger. It is not a bank, lender, money transmitter, or escrow service and does not custody roommate funds. Settlements, child allowances and rewards are records; they do not send money.

Does everyone see every activity?

No. Visibility follows the activity and its participants. Joining a household does not automatically make every expense or private conversation visible to the whole house.

What is Household Plus?

Household Plus is optional. It adds recurring routines, monthly check-ins and reusable handovers with moving checklists and private photos. Basic shared records and safety tools remain available without Plus.

The iOS offering includes monthly and annual auto-renewable subscriptions and a one-time Household Permanent purchase. A buyer owns coverage for one selected eligible household at a time. The purchase screen shows the applicable price and terms. See the Purchase and Subscription Policy for the complete terms, including cancellation and refunds.

Where are child-profile records kept?

Child profiles, optional photos, chores and recorded allowances stay encrypted on the family device. Roofmates retains none on its servers. Child profiles cannot make independent purchases or join the adult network. Guardian controls provide local deletion; Roofmates support cannot restore these records.

Is Roofmates available to download?

We are preparing the iOS release for App Store review. There is no public download link or announced release date yet.

How can someone get support?

Visit the Roofmates support page or email roofmates-support@cedarwavetechnologies.com. The Privacy Policy and Terms of Service explain the current service terms.
