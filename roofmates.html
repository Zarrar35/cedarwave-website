Boundaries

Coordinate what's shared.
Keep context intact.

Household membership does not automatically make every expense, plan, or conversation visible to everyone. Every activity in Roofmates belongs to one of six scopes, and visibility follows participation.

Roofmates does not custody roommate funds. Any person-to-person payment capability must use an approved provider and remains disabled until provider, compliance, production, and release gates pass.

HouseholdVisible to every current member of the house.
EventScoped to an event's participants and invited guests.
PoolShared with the people contributing to a pooled plan.
TransactionLimited to a single expense's participants.
PrivateYours alone, or a chosen few — never house-wide by default.
GuestLets non-members join an event without joining the house.

Questions

What Roofmates
is — and is not.

Who is Roofmates for?

Adults coordinating a shared household — roommates, friends, co-living houses, and people setting up a new home together.

Does Roofmates hold or transfer money?

Roofmates does not custody roommate funds. Provider-backed person-to-person payments are a launch goal, but that capability is disabled while provider eligibility, compliance, production, and release acceptance remain in progress.

Does everyone see every activity?

No. Visibility follows participation across household, Event, Pool, transaction, private, and guest contexts.

Which platforms are planned?

Roofmates is being prepared as a native app for iOS and Android.

How can someone get support?

Visit the Roofmates support page or email roofmates-support@cedarwavetechnologies.com.

What is planned for the public version?

The feature checklist covers shared expenses and receipts, Events and Pools, household operations and administration, participant-scoped messaging, Finder, Premium, narrow non-legal tenancy coordination, and person-to-person payments through an approved provider.

This is the intended launch scope, not a statement that every feature is live today.

What is still in progress before a public launch?

Live provider integrations for payments and Premium, production rollout, moderation and retention operations, signed final builds, legal and store review, and late physical-device acceptance remain open. No feature listed on this page should be read as currently available to the public.

Is there a Premium?

Premium is part of the planned public version. Store billing remains disabled until the product, provider, legal, store, and release gates pass.
